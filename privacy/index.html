Little Room privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026. This policy describes the Little Room iPad app and its external TestFlight beta, maintained by the Little Room team. Contact 723156735@qq.com.

Reading data on your device

Reading progress, favorites, completed books, text highlights, PDF drawings and activity progress are stored on your device. Little Room does not upload these records to its library backend. Local data remains until you clear it in an activity or remove the app. Device backups may retain copies and are controlled through your device and backup settings.

Camera, microphone and activity recordings

Some learning activities request camera or microphone access after you choose the relevant action. Camera frames are processed on your device. Activity recordings stay on your device and are not uploaded by Little Room. Recording storage depends on the activity: temporary recordings are discarded when it closes, and any saved local progress remains on your device. Decline permission or revoke it in Settings at any time.

Library downloads and service logs

The app requests a content catalog and downloads pictures, audio, PDF files and learning activities from Tencent Cloud. These requests disclose your IP address and basic technical request information to the hosting services. Service and security logs are used to deliver content, diagnose failures and protect the service, not for advertising or cross-service tracking. Our configured cloud-function application logs are retained for 7 days; Tencent Cloud may retain its own infrastructure and security records under its service policies.

No advertising or accounts

Little Room has no user account registration, advertising, advertising identifiers or behavioral analytics SDK. We do not sell personal information or use it to build advertising profiles.

Support you choose to send

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use it to respond and address your request, and delete support correspondence when it is no longer needed, ordinarily within 90 days after resolution unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. Please do not send children's personal information unless necessary for a private privacy request.

Public GitHub feedback is visible to everyone and follows GitHub's privacy and retention policies. You can edit or remove your own public posts using GitHub's tools or contact us for help. Private concerns should be sent by email.

This website and TestFlight

This website uses no analytics, advertising, remote fonts or third-party embeds. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security. Opening a GitHub link or sending an email is optional. Apple separately processes TestFlight installation, crash and feedback information under Apple's TestFlight privacy terms.

Children and parent choices

The app is designed for family learning. We do not request children's names, dates of birth, contact information or accounts. A parent or guardian should manage permissions and send support requests. The in-app grown-up gate protects links leaving the app; it is not a request for consent to upload children's personal data.

Your choices and requests

You can decline capture permissions, stop capture by leaving an activity, clear progress where an activity offers that control, or remove the app to delete local app data. To ask about access, correction or deletion of support information, email us. We may ask for limited information necessary to verify a request. This policy will be updated if data practices change.
